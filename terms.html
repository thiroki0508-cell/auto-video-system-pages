Terms of Service

Terms for a personal research and original-content planning tool.

Effective and last updated: October 14, 2026

1. Purpose and access

Auto Video System is an individually developed, private Windows desktop application used by its owner in Japan. Its purpose is to research public YouTube metadata, organize candidates, and support the owner's original-content planning and production assumptions.

This public website provides application information and policies. It is not a hosted version of the desktop application and grants no public account, download, subscription, or access to the private code or local data.

2. YouTube and other services

Auto Video System uses YouTube API Services. By using the application, users agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's data practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy; the app's practices are described in its Privacy Policy.

Auto Video System is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by YouTube or Google. This website does not claim that the application, any analysis, or a quota or permission request has been approved by Google or YouTube.

The optional developing original-topic planner uses the owner's separate OpenAI API account. That provider's applicable terms, privacy practices, and charges apply. It is separate from the local YouTube review tools.

3. Respect for creators and third-party rights

Public availability does not transfer ownership or give permission to reuse a creator's videos, images, music, text, or trademarks. Respect copyright, privacy, platform rules, and all applicable permissions. The app supports research and original planning; it does not grant a license to third-party content.

The current app does not download YouTube videos, copy or republish them, or automatically upload content. Do not use references or generated suggestions to misrepresent ownership or bypass rights restrictions.

4. Research and planning limitations

Metadata can be incomplete, unavailable, or outdated between retrievals. Public counts do not prove audience demand, revenue, monetization eligibility, or suitability for inexpensive production. Basic local text rules and image measurements are review aids, not full-video understanding or authoritative evaluations.

Cost estimates reflect the owner's entered assumptions, not the source creator's actual spending or an actual provider bill. Optional generated outlines and suggestions require human fact-checking and rights review. No income, accuracy, availability, API quota, or monetization outcome is promised. Low-originality or repetitive production may be ineligible for monetization under platform rules.

5. Current operation and restrictions

Use only API credentials and access that you are authorized to use. Protect keys, respect provider request limits, and do not bypass access restrictions. Keep local backups secure and manage API-data copies within applicable retention rules. See the Privacy Policy for current retention and deletion behavior.

Numerical popularity/growth scoring, cross-channel performance evaluation, and actual revenue prediction are not enabled. End-to-end autonomous video production and posting are not implemented. The wider analysis workflow remains under development and may require further review or permission before additional uses are enabled.

6. Changes and contact

These terms may be revised as the private application changes. The effective date and public documentation history identify revisions. Publication is informational and does not by itself capture agreement within the desktop application.

General questions can be raised through the public documentation repository's GitHub Issues. Do not post keys, personal information, or private app data there.
